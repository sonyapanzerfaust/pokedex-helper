Playthrough route

Requirements and rules

Consoles for cartridge play

  • Two Nintendo DS or DS Lite systems with GBA slots. Use local wireless for DS trades, the Underground and the Ranger egg transfer. Track console progress manually.
  • DS systems run GBA cartridges in single-player mode. For the Kanto starters, migrate separately from each FireRed save: box the starter and five other Pokémon, keep one in the party and catch all six in Platinum’s Pal Park.
  • Game Boy Advance or GBA SP (optional). For direct trades between two FireRed cartridges, use two GBA or GBA SP systems and a GBA Game Link Cable. You can then migrate all three starters together through Pal Park.

Emulators for PC play

  • melonDS — for DS games. Trading requires two sessions with working local communication. For Pal Park, connect the GBA game and save to the emulated GBA slot.
  • mGBA — for playing GBA games. For automatic tracking, connect the .sav created by saving from the in-game menu.

Games used by the route

  • Destination · DS — Pokémon Platinum (USA). Register all 493 species here.
  • Donors · DS — Diamond, Pearl, HeartGold and SoulSilver. Use separate saves for additional starter runs.
  • GBA donors — Emerald and FireRed. LeafGreen is optional: FireRed offers Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle. Choose a different starter from Oak in each of three runs. With two copies, keep the main game and start the second donor run only after transferring the previous starter.
  • Optional Manaphy Egg route · DS — choose Pokémon Ranger, Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia or Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs. The route also offers a historical gift in Diamond.

Route rules

  1. Obtain ordinary species in-game by catching, breeding, evolving or trading. A donor catch counts once registered in the main Platinum save.
  2. Equip any item required for a trade evolution. For GBA migration, unlock the National Dex and Pal Park, migrate six Pokémon and catch them in the park. Migration is one-way.
  3. Expired distribution: a save editor or Action Replay may restore the historical gift or encounter access for a compatible game and region.
  4. Discontinued online service: restore only the requirement that depended on that service. A replacement server is optional. Spiritomb has a separate local-play exception below.
  5. Back up the save before editing. After restoring access, collect the gift or catch the Pokémon in-game and save from the game menu.

Mythical Pokémon and distributions

  • Darkrai — restore the Platinum Member Card distribution and complete the Newmoon Island encounter.
  • Shaymin — Oak’s Letter opens the path from Route 224 to Flower Paradise.
  • Arceus — use a released Wonder Card such as the Toys “R” Us gift. The Azure Flute was never officially distributed for the original Gen IV games.

Manaphy and Phione

  • Pokémon Ranger (US). Before starting, check whether the cartridge has already sent its egg. Each cartridge sends one egg, even if you start a new game.
  • Finish the story, unlock the Ranger Net Manaphy mission with the US password and complete it. Shadows of Almia and Guardian Signs each use a downloadable mission. Restoring its historical distribution is allowed.
  • Send the egg over local wireless to Diamond or Pearl on the second DS. Hatch Manaphy and trade it to Platinum. On PC, launch Ranger separately in melonDS and check local communication before the playthrough.
  • For Phione, leave Manaphy and Ditto at the Day Care and hatch their egg.

Spiritomb and the Underground

  1. Place the Odd Keystone in the Hallowed Tower on Route 209.
  2. Speak to another player in the Underground 32 times over DS local wireless. Leaving and re-entering lets the same partner count again. No internet connection is required.
  3. If local play is unavailable, you may set the interaction counter to 32. Then return to the tower and catch Spiritomb.
