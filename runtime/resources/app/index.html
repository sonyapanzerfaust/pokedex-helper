Playthrough route

Requirements

Games and saves

Collect all 493 species in your main Pokémon Platinum (USA) save. A catch in a donor game counts once that Pokémon reaches Platinum. Keep separate saves for extra starter runs.

Nintendo DS · melonDS. Play Platinum (USA) as the destination game. The DS donor stages use Diamond, Pearl, HeartGold and SoulSilver.

Game Boy Advance · mGBA. The GBA donor stages use Emerald, FireRed and LeafGreen. For migration, switch to the DS setup described below.

Playing and transferring

On PC, use melonDS for DS and mGBA for GBA, with your own game files. For automatic tracking, save from the in-game menu and connect the battery save (.sav). On a console, use manual tracking.

For version exclusives and trade evolutions, arrange a local trade between two compatible Gen IV games (two DS systems or emulator sessions with working local communication). Equip the required held item before trading.

For the GBA stages, unlock the National Dex and Pal Park in Platinum. Use a DS or DS Lite with the donor cartridge in the GBA slot, or configure its equivalent in melonDS with the GBA game and save. Migrate six Pokémon at a time, then catch them in Pal Park. Migration is one-way.

Optional spin-off for Manaphy

Pokémon Ranger (Nintendo DS, US version) offers an alternative to the Diamond event gift in the route. Before starting a used cartridge, check whether its Manaphy Egg has already been sent. Only one egg can be sent per cartridge, even after starting a new game.

Finish the main story, unlock the Manaphy mission in Ranger Net with the US password and complete it. Send the egg over local wireless to Diamond or Pearl on a second DS. Collect and hatch it there, then trade Manaphy to your main Platinum save. Breed Manaphy with Ditto for Phione.

Other DS options are Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia and Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs. Each requires its own downloadable Manaphy mission. If the distribution has ended, the route allows restoring the historical mission and completing it in-game. Choose one Ranger game. For emulator play, run Ranger separately in melonDS and check local communication with the receiving session before committing to the playthrough.

What the route allows

Catch, breed, evolve and trade ordinary species in-game. Before using a save editor or Action Replay, back up the save. Restore only the original gift or the missing condition described below. Finish the encounter or collect the gift in-game and save normally.

Expired distributions

Use a documented Gen IV distribution compatible with the game and region. In Platinum, the Member Card opens the encounter with Darkrai and Oak’s Letter opens the path to Shaymin. For Arceus, use a distributed gift such as the Toys “R” Us Wonder Card. The Azure Flute was never distributed for these DS games.

Discontinued services and Spiritomb

If a required online service has shut down, the route allows restoring its specific unlock with a save editor or Action Replay. You do not need to set up a replacement server. Local trades still work independently of Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection.

Spiritomb uses local DS wireless. Place the Odd Keystone in the Hallowed Tower on Route 209 and speak to another player 32 times in the Underground. The same partner counts after leaving and re-entering. If you cannot arrange local play, this route allows setting only the Underground interaction counter to 32. Return to the tower and catch Spiritomb yourself.
